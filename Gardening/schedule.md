## Notes

- "Best" dates are the recommended sub-window within the full planting window from the Almanac.
- Celery has no fall planting dates in the Almanac's fall calendar for this region. Cucumbers, dry beans, eggplants, marigolds, potatoes, tomatoes, winter squash, and zucchini also have no fall dates listed.
- Cool-season crops (kale, spinach, collards, broccoli, lettuce, Swiss chard) prefer cooler weather and may bolt in summer heat; plant spring crops early and fall crops late.
- Warm-season/tender crops (green beans, dry beans, cucumbers, tomatoes, eggplants, potatoes) must be planted after the last frost in spring.
