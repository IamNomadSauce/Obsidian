## Welcome to the Rat Rig Wiki

This wiki is the central hub for all step-by-step build guides for Rat Rig products. It is focused on clear, structured assembly instructions that take you from unboxing to a fully built machine.

Rat Rig documentation is currently split across two platforms, each with a distinct purpose:

- [wiki.ratrig.com](http://wiki.ratrig.com/) — Step-by-step build and assembly guides. Technical documentation, including STLs, BOMs, CAD files, commissioning guides, firmware files, and downloads
- [os.ratrig.com](http://os.ratrig.com/) — Documentation for RatOS, Rat Rig’s 3D-printer firmware

These platforms are designed to complement each other. Use the wiki to build, for technical reference, and the RatOS site for firmware setup and operation.
